Helpful assembly tips:
Make sure the holes for the 1.5mm wire are big enough (make sure to print using precise holes/polyholes in your slicer)
You can clean up the holes, and ensure free movement by placing a length of 1.5mm wire in a power drill, and drill through the holes. The finger paddles should spin freely. The two halves of the finger tower should be a friction fit with the 1.5mm wire (you don't want the wire to fall out)
